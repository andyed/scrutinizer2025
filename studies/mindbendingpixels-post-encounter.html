Moderator runbook

Keep the encounter fresh.

  1. Confirm the participant has just encountered the selected product.
  2. Enter only an anonymous participant code.
  3. Launch, then let the Scrutinizer interstitial carry each instruction.
  4. Avoid rescuing navigation. Ask neutral clarification questions only.
  5. After final Done, use the probes below.

Research boundary

“Done” records that the participant stopped; it does not establish task success. Adjudicate outcomes after reviewing the trail and session.

Post-session probes

  1. In your own words, what is Mind Bending Pixels?
  2. How does the product you encountered relate to the other work you saw?
  3. What, if anything, would you do next?
  4. What felt surprising, unclear, or out of place?
