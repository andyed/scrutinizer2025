# Hansen, Pracejus & Gegenfurtner (2009) — Methodological Audit

Date: 2026-03-04

## Paper
"Color perception in the intermediate periphery of the visual field."
*Journal of Vision* 9(4):26, 1-12. DOI: 10.1167/9.4.26

## Verdict
Solid threshold psychophysics for its intended question (does L-M opponency persist beyond 25-30 deg?).
**NOT an appearance study.** Our docs overcite it for "saturation retention" — it measured detection, not perceived saturation.

## Key Methodological Facts
- **Stimuli**: 5 deg and 8 deg uniformly colored disks on isoluminant gray
- **Apparatus**: Elumens VisionStation (hemispherical projection), LCD projector (Epson 730c), 1024x768
- **Color space**: DKL (cone-opponent), calibrated with PR650 spectroradiometer
- **Eccentricities**: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 deg
- **Duration**: 500 ms
- **Observers**: 5 naive (not all in all experiments); Figure 3 has N=3 chromatic, N=2 achromatic
- **Eye tracking**: NONE — fixation enforced by instruction only
- **Tasks**: ALL thresholds — detection (4AFC spatial), identification (4AFC color naming), discrimination (4AFC odd-one-out)

## Strengths
1. VisionStation gives uniform geometry to 50 deg (better than flat monitor)
2. Thorough space-variant calibration (gamma, spatial distortion, chromaticity)
3. DKL color space is physiologically appropriate
4. Convergent evidence from three task types
5. 50 deg eccentricity range exceeds most prior work

## Weaknesses
1. **Threshold, not appearance** — cannot tell us about suprathreshold saturation
2. **No eye tracking** — 500 ms allows reflexive saccades at near eccentricities
3. **Small N** — critical Figure 3 comparison has N=3; Figure 5 has N=1
4. **Isolated stimuli on gray** — no ecological validity for cluttered displays
5. **LCD projector gamut** narrower than CRT; 0.02 xy variation across sphere

## Correct vs. Incorrect Use in Our Docs
- WRONG: "large colored regions retain perceived saturation" (they didn't measure this)
- RIGHT: "cone-opponent mechanisms persist to 50 deg with appropriately sized stimuli"
- For saturation retention, cite Jiang et al. 2022, Tyler 2015, or Rosenholtz/TTM instead

## Relationship to Bowers, Gegenfurtner & Goettker (2025)
Same lab, 16 years later. Bowers et al. validates the core finding (L-M opponency persists)
and extends it with better quantitative characterization showing biphasic (non-exponential) RG decay.
