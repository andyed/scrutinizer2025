Figure N: Cortical sampling geometry. Ring boundaries from uniform cortical sampling (w = log(r + a), a = 2.78°). (a) Gaussian blur has no discrete pooling regions. (b) Polar grid with fixed spoke count: outer cells stretch into arcs (red). (c) FOVI-derived isotropic grid: spoke count adapted per ring for locally square cells in cortical coordinates (green).
