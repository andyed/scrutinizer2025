This technique works by generating a field of tiny rotating crosses at a scale tuned to be just resolvable by your fovea.

The crosses are small enough (~0.13 units) and numerous enough (15 layered fields) that your peripheral vision can't resolve them individually—they blur into grayish noise.

"Wherever your fovea lands, suddenly the structure pops into sharp, colorful detail."

The pattern exists uniformly everywhere, but your retina does the blurring.
