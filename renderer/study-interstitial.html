Before you start

Taking part in this session

You will try a few short tasks on websites. We are testing the design, not you. There are no wrong answers.

What Scrutinizer does

It keeps the area around your mouse pointer clear and blurs everything farther away, the way your peripheral vision loses detail. It does not use a camera and does not track your eyes.

What this computer records during the tasks

  • Mouse movement, clicks, scrolling, and actions like copy and paste, with timestamps.
  • The address of each page you visit, and a screenshot of it. A screenshot shows everything on the page, including anything you have typed there.
  • Key presses. Inside a text box it records only that a key was pressed, never which key or what you typed. Outside text boxes it records which key.
  • How long each task takes, your screen size, and the tool's settings.

Nothing is recorded on screens like this one. The recording is saved on this computer under a participant code, not your name, and Scrutinizer does not upload it.

Other recordings

The moderator may also take notes or record video or audio with their own equipment. They will tell you what they record, who is running the study, and how long data is kept. You can say no.

Your choice

The blurred view can feel odd or tiring. You can take a break or stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your recording to be deleted.
